# Course plan — A1 → B1

62 lessons in 16 units, written for a complete beginner. The lesson list itself lives in [course.js](course.js) — the home page shows it, with unwritten lessons marked "coming soon".

## How every lesson works

1. **Watch** — a board-lecture video (6–8 min): the teacher explains in English, French is spoken by a French voice, the board fills in step by step. Ends with *À vous !* — say it out loud before the answer appears.
2. **Notes** — the written version of the video, for revising.
3. **Words** — 15–25 words or phrases with audio; nouns show their gender.
4. **Dialogue** — a short real-life conversation using the lesson's language, two voices, with a hide-the-English mode.
5. **Practice** — recognise first (choose, listen), then produce (write it, build the sentence).
6. **With your tutor** — 3–4 things to practise aloud in the next live (Rosetta Stone) session.

A1 lessons each include a **sound of the lesson**, so pronunciation is built up one sound at a time instead of front-loaded:

| Lesson | Sound |
|---|---|
| 1 Bonjour ! | silent final letters, *c-r-f-l* |
| 2 Je m'appelle… | the French R |
| 3 L'alphabet et les nombres | *u* vs *ou* |
| 4 Un, une, le, la | nasal vowels |
| Unit 2 onwards | the three E sounds, liaison, silent verb endings, question intonation… |

## Levels

- **A1 Découverte** — Units 1–6, 24 lessons. Greetings → introductions → numbers → gender → être/avoir/-er verbs → questions → describing → time → getting around → food and shopping → daily routine. Ends with a review lesson.
- **A2 Fondations** — Units 7–11, 20 lessons. Past tenses, pronouns, comparing, future, linking ideas, conditional.
- **B1 Indépendance** — Units 12–16, 18 lessons. Subjunctive, narrating, complex sentences, reporting and arguing, real spoken French.
